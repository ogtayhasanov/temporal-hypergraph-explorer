    INPUT, PROCESS AND OUTPUT

    How the hierarchy is constructed

    The input is an already extracted knowledge hypergraph. The algorithm groups entities using their recorded relations and, after the first snapshot, their earlier group memberships.

    INPUT

    A dated hypergraph

    • Entity IDs and names
    • Multi-entity relation records
    • Each relation’s source paper and publication year

    For cutoff t, retain relations asserted by papers published by t. Their endpoints define the current entity universe.

    →

    ALGORITHM

    1. Make structural featuresBuild the node–relation incidence matrix and compress it with SVD, to rank at most 48.
    2. Initialize and refine fine groupsUse k-means or earlier memberships. Move a unit only when its exact full objective gain is positive.
    3. Contract and repeatBuild L1 from complete L2 groups, then L0 from complete L1 groups. A coarser move cannot split a child.
    4. Track and labelMatch group identities across snapshots and attach labels from the eligible source evidence.
    →

    OUTPUT FOR 2026

    A nested map

    15 broad groups L0
    60 middle groups L1
    221 fine groups L2
    5,798 entity leaves L3

    Also exported: labels, persistent group IDs, member flows and events.

    What earns a higher score?

    A relation earns observed credit when one group holds strictly more than half of its original endpoints. Modularity subtracts the expected credit under a degree-volume chance model. The selected objective adds a memory modularity term weighted by 0.25.

    Refinement stops after a sweep accepts no moves, or after four sweeps. This is finite greedy search.

    What survives contraction?

    If five endpoints are split between two groups as 3 and 2, the contracted relation stores those same counts and the original arity 5. Its observed credit stays 3/5 for the same grouping. Degree volumes also remain unchanged sums, so the full objective agrees.

    Internal relations remain as loops. The guarantee applies to this objective for corresponding partitions.

    Group caps: L0 ≤ 15 · L1 ≤ 60 · L2 ≤ 300Selected weights: κ = 0 · τ = 0.25Initialization seed: 42

    The caps are maxima for the number of groups, not the number of entities in a group. The 2020 snapshot has no preceding-group memory. Display labels reproduce the frozen output, including broad groups marked “Mixed”; the visualization supplies no additional semantic validation.
